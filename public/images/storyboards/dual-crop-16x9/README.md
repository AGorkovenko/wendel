# Wendel video keyframes — 16:9

Seven PNG images, each exactly 1920 × 1080, numbered in story order. Use consecutive pairs as the start and end frames for six video transitions.

The original panoramic files remain unchanged in `../dual-crop/`.

Preparation: built-in image generation extended the sky and foreground vertically while retaining both crops and the branded packaging. The generated images were then resized to a uniform 1920 × 1080 with macOS sips. The source generation was approximately 1672 × 941; the delivered 1080p files are resized exports, not native 1080p generations. Generated extensions can vary in clouds and small scene details between frames.

## Shared generation prompt

Convert the supplied panoramic photo to a 16:9 landscape canvas. Preserve the entire original photo width without cropping either side or stretching. Place the original scene across the middle 74% of the canvas height, adding approximately 13% above and below. Outpaint the softly clouded dawn sky above and soil, path, crops and objects below. Retain the mountain silhouette, sun, horizon, central path, camera angle, crop stages, hands where present, packaging and Wendel logos. Match the original exposure, warm sunrise and photographic textures. No borders, blur bands, text overlays or new objects.
