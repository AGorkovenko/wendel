# Wendel homepage — field to box

## Design plan

Palette: studio white #ffffff, Wendel green #08763f, leaf ink #173b29, soft leaf #e9efdf, berry #b72742, muted text #647065.

Type: Instrument Sans for large, closely set medium-weight headlines and body copy. Original Wendel lettering is reserved for the logo. No decorative serif treatment.

Layout: a full-viewport film with left-aligned opening copy; its final studio frame contracts into a centered brand composition. Below, an open three-column harvest section, a large field photograph paired with a green visit panel, and practical contact information.

    [fullscreen field + opening headline]
    [pinned video controlled by scroll  ]
    [          Wendel logo             ]
    [     final boxes become smaller   ]
    [      three seasonal products     ]
    [large field photograph | visit    ]
    [      farm address / contacts     ]

Review: removed the previous decorative crop drawings, generic statistic tiles and cream/serif styling. The distinctive moment is the actual field-to-packaging film, followed by the real logo above its final boxes. Surrounding sections stay quiet and useful.

## Motion and delivery

The film occupies the full viewport until its last frame. A separate final scroll interval shrinks that frame and reveals the logo. Scrolling backward reverses both phases. Native sticky positioning, no wheel interception. Reduced-motion and media-error modes use the final still and remove the long scroll runway. A visible skip link lets visitors reach the content directly.

Source video is preserved. The website uses a silent 1600px-wide 30fps derivative with short GOPs for seeking, plus extracted first/last stills. Portrait screens fill the viewport with a centered crop during the field sequence. The final frame always fits completely so all three boxes remain visible.
