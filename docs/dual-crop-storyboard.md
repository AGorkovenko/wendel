# Dual-crop scroll story

Seven locked-composition, full-screen frames for a future scroll-driven sequence. In every frame, white asparagus is on the left and strawberries are on the right.

## Agricultural sequence

1. **Planting** — asparagus crowns are placed in a prepared trench; strawberry plug plants are planted into clean soil.
2. **Establishment** — young asparagus fern develops above the buried crown; strawberry plants root and build leaf mass.
3. **Ridges and protection** — mature asparagus beds are formed into high ridges and covered with reversible film; strawberry rows receive drip irrigation and low crop protection.
4. **Underground growth / blossom** — white asparagus shoots elongate inside the dark ridge while strawberries flower and attract pollinators.
5. **Harvest readiness** — a fine crack or emerging tip signals an asparagus spear; strawberry fruit progresses from green through blush to red.
6. **Hand harvest** — asparagus is exposed and cut below the surface with a specialist knife; ripe strawberries are picked by the stem into cardboard punnets.
7. **Fresh from the field** — asparagus is aligned in kraft cardboard produce boxes; strawberries are presented in cardboard punnets. Both carry Wendel branding based on the official logo. This is a packaging concept, not a verified reproduction of the company's existing boxes.

The important difference is that marketable white asparagus is a shoot harvested before it reaches light. It does not pass through a flowering stage during the harvest story. After the traditional end of harvest on 24 June, remaining shoots are allowed to develop into tall green fern so the crown can rebuild carbohydrate reserves for the following season.
